Scheduling Logic

  1. Patients enter a priority queue.
  2. Highest-priority patients are considered first.
  3. Available resources are checked against constraints.
  4. The earliest valid time slot is selected.
  5. Local-search refinement attempts to move assignments earlier.
  6. The dashboard reports utilisation, waiting time and makespan.
